## The NLP Landscape from 1960s to 2026

**NLP Landscape Overview**:
    - The evolution of Natural Language Processing (NLP) from 1960 to 2026, highlighting key milestones and advancements.
    - Key areas include:
        - Rule-based approaches in the 1960s-1980s
        - Machine learning and statistical models in the 1990s-2000s
        - Deep learning techniques from 2010 onwards
        - Current trends such as transformers, attention mechanisms, and multimodal processing
**Notable Developments**: The notable developments includes the development of ELIZA in 1966, IBM's Watson in 2007, and BERT in 2018.
