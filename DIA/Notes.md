#### Image Pyramid for Image resolution
![image pyramid](images/image_pyramid.png)
```
Original
512×512
   ↓
Blur + ↓2
256×256
   ↓
Blur + ↓2
128×128
   ↓
Blur + ↓2
64×64


Here, ↓2 = downsampling
```
