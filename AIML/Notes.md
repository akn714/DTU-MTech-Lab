| Point | Batch Gradient Descent | Stochastic Gradient Descent |
|---|---|---|
| **Data used** | Uses the entire dataset to calculate each update. | Uses one training example at a time. |
| **Speed** | **Slow** — slower per update, especially for large datasets. | **Fast** — makes frequent and faster updates. |
| **Memory required** | **High** — needs to process the entire dataset at once. | **Low** — processes one example at a time. |
| **Accuracy of updates** | More stable and smooth updates. | Updates are noisy and fluctuate more. |
| **Convergence** | Usually converges smoothly toward a minimum. | Can fluctuate around the minimum but may reach a good solution faster. |
